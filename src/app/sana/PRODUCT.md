# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

> Scope: SANA and its public landing at `/sana/` only. This codebase hosts multiple products; this record does not define the GREENATICS institutional site, GREENATICS Red, Calcula tu Huella, AgroWay, or OPS.

## Platform
web

## Users
Project promoters and organizations structuring agricultural or productive initiatives; allies and prospective investors who may review a project through a human-led process.

## Product Purpose
SANA is an ecosystem in development for organizing agroproductive projects and connecting them with potential allies or investors through human review. Success requires a clear project context, scope, responsibilities, assumptions, and a considered next step; it does not imply that a project is funded or operating.

## Positioning
SANA frames project structuring and participation as a documented, human-reviewed process rather than an automated investment decision. The current public sample demonstrates the idea with fictional data; it is not a live investment platform.

## Operating Context
People assess a project by its productive context, stage, actors, intended scope, resources, responsibilities, assumptions, and follow-up. Any request to express interest remains subject to human review.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- A local conceptual sample can be explored with fictional project information.
- The sample does not create a real portfolio, accept or transfer investments, make automated decisions, or persist/share visitor input.
- No real project, client, operating deployment, payment, or investment outcome is established by available evidence.
- Blockchain, tokenization, automated investment, projected returns, and economic outcomes must not be claimed from older brand materials or a fictional dossier.

## Brand Commitments
SANA is distinct from GREENATICS institutional brand and AgroWay. The user has directed that SANA’s public landing not inherit GREENATICS tokens or composition. No validated standalone SANA logo or approved landing image is available; do not reconstruct a logo, substitute another brand’s mark, or reuse an unapproved asset.

## Product Principles
- Make project status and assumptions explicit.
- Explain human review and responsibilities before inviting participation.
- Distinguish a fictional demonstration from a real operating service.
- Avoid financial promises and unsupported claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Use clear language, predictable navigation, and keyboard-operable interactions. Do not claim accessibility certification without evidence.
